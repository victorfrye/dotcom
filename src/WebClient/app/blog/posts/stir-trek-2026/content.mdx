Thank you to the greater Columbus developer community for an exceptional Stir Trek 2026! This was a conference recommended to me multiple times and finally got invited this year. It was such a pleasure to be invited and present to the community here.

This blog post is a reference for those who attended my presentations as well as a thank you letter to everyone involved.

## The DevOops Handbook

![DevOops Handbook hero](/assets/_blog/stir-trek-2026/devoops_handbook.jpg)

My presentation was "The DevOops Handbook" and is always a favorite to present. The session focused on satirically talking about all the mistakes we frequently make in the software development lifecycle for the reasons DevOps is so important, but are antithetical to the principles of DevOps. We talked about the three ways: masochistic deployments, project mismanagement, and the quality void. The slides and additional resources are available below:

- [Presentation slide deck](https://github.com/victorfrye/presentations/blob/main/files/2026/stirtrek/devoopshandbook.pdf)
- [Session feedback](https://forms.office.com/r/6ChG8mUTv8)
